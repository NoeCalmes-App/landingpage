# Notes App Review — modèle à compléter en anglais

Retirer les champs sans objet en indiquant brièvement pourquoi lorsque c'est utile. Ne jamais envoyer ce modèle avec des champs non remplis. Pour un refus existant, répondre d'abord dans l'ordre exact des questions d'Apple. Ne déclarer que les appareils effectivement testés et les comportements vérifiés.

## Publisher and app purpose

- Legal publisher: [client legal entity].
- App/brand and relationship to the publisher: [explanation].
- This is [the first submission / another submission] from this organization account.
- The app helps [audience] to [specific task].
- Intended distribution and user eligibility: [public audience / actual restrictions].
- Development agency, if relevant: [agency acting for the publisher; rights or authorization if requested].
- Business model: [who pays, for what, where, and how access is granted].

## Review access and walkthrough

- Demo credentials: supplied in the App Review Information fields.
- Account roles and additional review access: [instructions; credentials outside Git].
- Setup, authentication codes, sample QR codes or hardware: [details, or not applicable].
- Main workflow: [numbered taps from first launch to the useful result].
- Account deletion, if applicable: [exact navigation and expected outcome/timing].
- Purchases/subscriptions, if applicable: [where products appear, how to test and restore].
- Required services and backend availability: [facts verified for this build].

## Demonstration and testing

- Submitted version/build: [version and build].
- Recording: [attachment or reviewer-accessible link; no login or expiring access barrier].
- Recording device and OS: [physical device model and actual OS version].
- Other devices/platforms tested: [actual list, dates and results].
- Special conditions demonstrated: [location, accessories, permissions or other relevant features].

## External services and availability

- External APIs/SDKs and purpose: [actual services used; explain non-obvious dependencies].
- User data sent to third parties and user controls, when relevant: [brief factual explanation].
- Country/region differences: [actual differences; explicitly state none only if verified].
- Licences, third-party rights or regulated activity: [relevant evidence, otherwise not applicable].
- Support and privacy links: [working URLs].

## When replying to an existing information request

For each Apple question: quote its number or short subject, give the direct answer, and identify the supporting attachment or exact navigation path. Explain whether the answer applies to the existing build or to a corrected build. Do not claim that a build changed when only notes were updated.
